Calendar

Add your calendar's private iCal link (it ends in .ics). Google: Calendar settings → your calendar → "Secret address in iCal format". Outlook: Settings → Shared calendars → Publish → ICS. iPhone/iCloud: share the calendar as a Public Calendar and copy the link.

Sports

Display

Rotary knob (KY-040)

Wired to the 8-pin header. Turn to move between Clock, Music, News and Settings. Press: next face (Clock), play/pause (Music), scroll headlines (News), brightness (Settings). Hold on Music for volume. In scroll/volume/brightness mode, press again to go back to changing screens.

Knob test (live)

Turn or press the knob and watch the values. At rest all three read high; turning should flick CLK and DT to low, pressing makes SW low. If nothing ever changes, the knob's GND wire isn't connected.

Update

Upload firmware.bin (from .pio/build/clockhub/) to update the watch over Wi-Fi. Upload littlefs.bin to update the web page and built-in faces. Updating the web page replaces the file system, so it also clears your settings and installed faces.
